The quest path

Five stamps
to a decision

Every application moves forward through five stages. Each move is a transaction, so the block stamps the time. It only goes forward, and it can't be backdated.

  1. 01

    Drafting

    You found it. It's on the board and the deadline clock is running.

  2. 02

    Submitted

    Sent. The timestamp shows you beat the deadline.

  3. 03

    Shortlisted

    You made the cut, out of the pile and onto the list.

  4. 04

    Interview

    Face to face. Show them who's behind the paper.

  5. 05

    Decision

    The waiting room. Whatever happens next, the work is on record.

  6. 🏆 / ✕

    Won or not this time

    Lock the result. Wins and misses both count as proof you showed up.
